## What the gallery records

Click an icon to open its inspector. Choose Ready or Revise and enter a comment. Export review JSON to keep a portable record. The browser's local review storage is best-effort and is not the project's canonical approval log. The manifest remains a review draft until a maintainer records an approval.

## Later testing

Prototype the chosen set in actual application components and test keyboard navigation, assistive technology, operating-system forced colors, zoom, disabled states, localization and high-DPI rendering. Test domain-specific recognition with researchers rather than assuming a clear drawing guarantees a clear meaning.
