# Batch 3 design and provenance notes

## Authority and continuity

The supplied `PrismQDA Icon List.md` determines the names, family assignments, requested metaphors and batch scope. The earlier PrismQDA packs provide the stroke and silhouette vocabulary. These new assets are original hand-authored paths, not raster tracing or third-party icon imports. No icon-set license attribution is introduced by this batch. The source inventory is retained unchanged.

## Family decisions

| Icons | Design treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Social media post | Speech bubble with one text mark and a separate bent reply/thread line. |
| Geodata | Plain map pin, without importing the colorful brand mark into a source icon. |
| Similarity | Two overlapping sheets with an approximate-equality sign on the front sheet. |
| Topics | Three clusters of three points; no enclosing group circles. |
| Classifier | A sheet points to two open bins. Small strokes label bins without introducing alphabetic text. |
| Sentiment | Minus and plus marks above a slider; shape, not color, conveys polarity. |
| Named entities | A word-shaped stroke in a rounded label box, with a small location pin. No literal letters. |
| Keyness | Two text columns, with one word-shaped capsule raised above its column. |
| Scales and reliability | Three aligned equal-length items grouped by a right-hand bracket. |
| Factor analysis | Two downward arrows each organize three lower points. |
| Cluster analysis | Two separate enclosing circles each contain three points, distinguishing clustering from the Topics icon. |
| Foot pedal | Sloped pedal surface with two grip marks and a base edge. |
| Synchronized | Two opposing circular arrows, without a checkmark implying an approved research result. |
| Offline | Plug and cable with a diagonal slash. Its appearance must follow the application's actual connection state. |

The regular icon family remains `fill="none"`. Small outlined dots become visually solid at small sizes because of the required 1.75-unit stroke; no extra fill colors are introduced. Compact word/item capsules use a radius of 1.5 where a radius of 2 cannot fit the requested height, continuing the prior compact-shape interpretation. Standard frames retain radius 2.

The brief's three/four-detail guidance is treated as a semantic-density goal, as in the earlier packs. Multi-point analytical symbols necessarily use multiple subpaths. Topics, Classifier, Factor Analysis and Cluster Analysis should retain visible labels in the working UI. Their different geometry is not a substitute for the label.

## Brand preservation

This batch creates no new brand assets. The consolidated package copies all earlier canonical SVGs without changing their bytes. The main dark-lettered horizontal logo is shown against white in the full gallery and preview image so its lettering remains visible; its source file is not recolored. Supplemental light-lettered and compact-mark files remain separately identified.

## Review state

All new assets are delivered as draft-for-review. No gallery Ready flag has been preselected. Review annotations are a convenience for the owner, not automatic release certification.
